Privacy Policy – NIHONGO CUP

Last updated: 2026-11-26

NIHONGO CUP ("the app") is developed and operated by KAI IWAO ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

1. Information the app stores on your device

Your game progress (matches played, scores, missed questions), your settings (language, furigana, sound, haptics) and the flag you chose. This data is stored only on your device. We do not have an account system and we cannot see this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

2. Information shared with third parties

Google Play Billing / RevenueCat (only when you make or restore a purchase): purchase tokens and an anonymous app-user identifier are processed by RevenueCat, Inc. to validate your purchase. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. RevenueCat's policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

Analytics: the current version sends no analytics. If we add usage analytics in a future version, this policy and the Google Play Data safety section will be updated first.

The app shows no advertising and contains no advertising SDK.

3. Permissions

The app requests no special permissions. Vibration (haptics) uses the standard system feature and can be turned off in Settings.

4. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

5. Your choices

  • Reset all progress: Settings → Reset progress.
  • Remove all data: uninstall the app.
  • Manage or cancel purchases: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

6. Contact

iwaokaiwo@gmail.com

7. Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above.
